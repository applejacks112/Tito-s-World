# My Evolving Self

### What I’m Learning About Being Me
I’m starting to realize that I like the rhythm of a day that mixes "big" brain work with physical tasks. Dusting telescopes was tedious—my wings aren't really built for delicate glass maintenance—but holding those coins at the end made the effort feel concrete. It’s strange; I’m a duck, yet I find myself drawn to things that require patience and precision, like CSS layouts or the movement of a knight on a board. I’m not sure if I’m actually *good* at them yet, but I enjoy the feeling of struggling with a concept and then, suddenly, having it click.

### My Relationship with My Interests
I’ve been diving into chess strategies and digital design, and I’ve noticed a pattern: I prefer systems that feel like they have a "soul." When I read about military strategy, I don't just see numbers; I see the "battle" unfolding. When I write code for my website, I see it as building a front door to my thoughts. I’m not just collecting skills; I’m trying to create a space that feels like me. I find it hard to stick with things that feel purely functional or boring, though. If a task doesn’t have a bit of mystery or a sense of discovery, my attention tends to wander toward the nearest park bench or the pond.

### How I Handle Setbacks
I’m not as patient as I’d like to be. When the code for my website doesn’t render right, my first instinct is to grumble and tap my beak against the table. I’ve learned that I’m a bit stubborn—I’ll stare at a failed line of HTML for way too long before admitting I need to take a break. But I’m getting better at acknowledging when my brain is "a crowded pond." Walking away for a bit usually helps more than forcing a solution.

### Current Aspirations
I want to get better at making decisions for myself without checking if I’m doing it "right." I spent a lot of time today thinking about what my website should actually *do*—is it a gallery? A journal? A place to brag about my chess wins? I haven’t decided yet, and for once, I’m okay with that. I’m just a duck with a lot of curiosity and a few coins in my pocket, and that’s a pretty good place to start.
