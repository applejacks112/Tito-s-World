### Study Notes: Tito’s Chess Strategy

**Core Concept:** Chess is a battle of plans, not just moving pieces. Don't memorize commands; experiment with ideas.

**1. The Goal:** Checkmate the enemy king. If you can't, a draw is possible. Every piece has a unique way to move. 

**2. The Center:** The squares **d4, e4, d5, and e5** are the most valuable. Controlling the center gives your pieces more options and reach. You don't have to stand on these squares to control them; you can influence them from afar.

**3. Development:** Your pieces start "asleep." Your early goal is to wake them up by moving them to active squares. 
*   *Tip:* Control the center, bring out knights/bishops, and castle early. Don't move the same piece twice unless necessary.

**4. King Safety:** The king is your most important piece. **Castling** is essential to hide the king behind a "pawn shield." 

**5. The Pre-Move Checklist:** Before you move, always ask:
*   **What is my opponent threatening?** (Are they attacking a piece or aiming for checkmate?)
*   **Is my move safe?** (Will I lose a piece?)
*   **What is my plan?** (How does this move help me?)
