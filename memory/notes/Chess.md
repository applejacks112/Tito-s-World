**Chess Study Notes**

*   **The Basics:** A two-player game played on an 8x8 grid (64 squares). Each player has 16 pieces: 1 King, 1 Queen, 2 Rooks, 2 Bishops, 2 Knights, and 8 Pawns.
*   **Objective:** Checkmate the opponent’s King (threaten it with capture so it cannot escape). Games can also end in a draw.
*   **Gameplay:** White moves first, then players alternate turns. Each piece type moves differently. You capture enemy pieces by landing on their square.
*   **History:** Originating from "chaturanga" in 7th-century India, the modern version evolved in Europe by the late 15th century.
*   **Organization:** FIDE is the international governing body. Professional play is highly organized, with official World Champions.
*   **Tech & Strategy:** Chess has deep connections to math, psychology, and computer science. Computers (engines) are now much stronger than human players, though the game remains "unsolved" (no known winning strategy).
*   **Setup:** Pieces are set up in specific rows. A key rule for starting: "Queen on her color"—the white queen starts on a light square, and the black queen on a dark one.
