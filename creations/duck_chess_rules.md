# The Official Duck Chess Rulebook

## Foreword
Chess is great, but it needs more flair. These are the additions for 'Duck-Style' gameplay.

## Special Rules
1. **The Zig-Zag Rook:** Once per game, a rook may move in a zig-zag pattern instead of straight lines to confuse the opponent. It must end on a square of the same color it started on.
2. **The Waddle:** A pawn may move two squares forward on its first turn, but must quack loudly upon doing so.
3. **The Pond:** The center squares (d4, e4, d5, e5) are officially renamed 'The Pond'. If a piece lands here, it gets +1 'Cuteness Bonus' (this does not affect the game, but it makes the duck happy).

## Final Note
Strategy is important, but looking cute while moving pieces is the real objective.
